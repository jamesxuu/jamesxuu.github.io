About Me

I am a PhD student in Quantitative Bioscience in the School of Physics at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Daniel I. Goldman. I am also pursuing an MS in Robotics/Computer Science.

My research uses physical experiments, robot design, and modeling to understand how multi-legged systems sense, adapt, and move through complex environments. I am especially interested in tactile sensing, terrain interaction, and locomotion across land and water.

Before Georgia Tech, I completed an MS in Biomedical Engineering at Columbia University and a BEng in Electrical Engineering at University College London.
