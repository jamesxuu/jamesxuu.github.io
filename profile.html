About Me

I am a PhD student in Quantitative Bioscience in the School of Physics at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Daniel I. Goldman. I am also pursuing an MS in Robotics/Computer Science.

My research focuses on locomotion in complex environments (terrestrial and aquatic). I study biological systems to understand how behavior emerges and how these insights can inform robot design.

Before Georgia Tech, I completed an MS in Biomedical Engineering at Columbia University and a BEng in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at University College London.
